# Candidate Evaluation Framework

## Technical Skills
- Core technologies
- Depth of knowledge
- Relevant project experience

## Communication
- Clarity of thought
- Problem-solving approach
- Professionalism

## Role Fit
- Experience alignment
- Industry exposure
- Team compatibility

## Hiring Considerations
- Notice period
- Location
- Compensation expectations
- Career goals

## Final Recommendation
- Strong Fit
- Potential Fit
- Not a Fit
