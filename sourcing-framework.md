# Candidate Sourcing Framework

## Step 1: Understand Role
Read job description carefully and identify:
- Skills required
- Experience level
- Must-have vs good-to-have

## Step 2: Search Candidates
Use:
- Boolean search
- LinkedIn search
- X-ray search

## Step 3: Shortlist Profiles
Check:
- Skills match
- Relevant experience
- Current role

## Step 4: Connect
Send short, clear message:
- Introduce role
- Ask for interest
